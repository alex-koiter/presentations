## Building teaching materials with Quarto

*November 20, 2025*

This short presentation (22 minutes) is part of the Take22 series hosted by the [Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology](https://www.brandonu.ca/ctlt/) at Brandon University.



[**View presentation**](http://alexkoiter.ca/presentations/Quarto.html)

## Abstract
Are you tired of using the Microsoft suite of tools to make slides and assignments for your courses and workshops? Do you want to make your teaching materials reproducible, easier to update, and in various formats? If so, maybe consider giving Quarto a try! Join Alex as he gives a brief overview of Quarto, an open-source scientific and technical publishing system, and shares his experience in creating teaching materials using Quarto.
